Design Process

How Confinium Mono developed from an archived 697-unit experiment into the current 800-unit Grid family. The studies show what changed, what was adopted, and what is still under review.

Latest experiment: Midline Knees · Current family review: Reading-size proof

1. 697-Unit Variable Experiment 697 advance · 100–700 wght

Tested an upright geometric monospace with 1,000 UPM, 697-unit advance, 700 cap height, 500 x-height, and Thin / Book / Bold masters for H O 0 Q g y c e s.

  • 697-Unit Experiment Specimen
  • Original v1 Nine-Glyph Sketch (SVG) · Updated Nine-Glyph Sketch (SVG)

2. Analytic Curve Defect Corrections c / e / s / g

Visual inspection and curve-continuity checks caught degenerate handles and drifting inner corner centres in c, e, s, and g. Outlines were rebuilt using analytic tangent geometry and affine-compatible construction so tangency holds across interpolated weights.

  • Curves Before Correction (PNG) vs Curves After Correction (PNG)

3. Grid & Token-Driven Bold Direction Study 800 advance · 1u = 80

Explored an 800 × 800 cell with 160-unit vertical stems, 160-unit outer radii, and non-concentric tight inner fillets across three coupled treatments (Grid, Open, Dense) for H O 0 Q C E S G Y.

  • Grid / Open / Dense Specimen · Enlarged C/E/S/G Detail
  • Grid Overlays: Grid · Open · Dense

4. Right-Descending 45° Q-Tail Direction Q45 comparison

Evaluated replacing the centred vertical Q tail with a 45° right-descending tail ending at y = −120 with a horizontal cut. The proof pairs the archived diagonal with a centred-tail reconstruction on the same bowl; the original centred binary is not in the repository’s history.

  • Centred vs 45° Right-Descending Q-Tail

5. Optical Diagonal Thickness & Baseline Promotion Q = 128 · Y = 144

Compared 128, 144, and 160 normal units for the 45° Q tail and Y arms against the lighter reference. Selected Q = 128 and Y = 144 independently and promoted both to the Grid & Token-Driven Bold baseline.

  • Optical Diagonals Comparison (128 / 144 / 160) · Enlarged O/Q/H/Y Detail
  • Grid Overlays: 128 · 144 · 160

6. ASCII Punctuation Grid Bold · 32 additions

Added all printable ASCII punctuation and operators in a separate Grid Bold build. The study proofs them in code and paths at 12, 16, and 28 px without changing the baseline.

  • Open Punctuation Study

7. From Uppercase to Mixed Text 95 printable ASCII characters

Built a lowercase alphabet with a 560-unit x-height, then combined it with the figures and punctuation to proof code and mixed-case text in the same 800-unit cell.

  • Lowercase foundations · Full lowercase alphabet · Mixed-text proof

8. European Latin and East Slavic Cyrillic

Extended coverage to European Latin and the modern Russian, Ukrainian, and Belarusian alphabets. Above-cap marks needed taller line metrics; supported combining marks keep zero advance.

  • European Latin proof · East Slavic Cyrillic proof

9. Full Grid Family 416 mapped characters

Grid, Open, Dense, and Hollow share the expanded repertoire. The 800-unit variable font covers weights 100–700 and matches Full Grid at Bold. Thin and Book are generated constructions, not independently refined masters.

  • Four static styles · Variable weight comparison · Try the current family

10. Reading-Size Review 12–18 px · Thin / Book / Bold

Compared code and mixed Latin/Cyrillic text in light and dark. The first priorities are distinguishing O from 0 in Book and making Thin punctuation read more evenly.

  • Programming ligatures: arrows and comparisons
  • Reading-size proof · Archived equal-stroke m study · Archived equal-width i study

11. Midline Knees Isolated Grid Bold experiment

Tested whether A and V would feel more at home on the grid if their diagonals started at the midline. A keeps vertical lower legs; V keeps vertical upper arms. The 800-unit advance and sidebearings are unchanged. The 152-unit follow-up replaces flared joins with parallel inner diagonals that meet the vertical walls directly: above the midline in V, below it in A. A’s crossbar moves down to clear the join. W adds the same direct join at a narrower 128-unit stroke to keep its two valleys open. The approved straight joins for A, V, W, v and the fuller-arm w are now in the full family, including accented forms and the shared Cyrillic А. Y retains its earlier approved construction.

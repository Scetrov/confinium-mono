Design Process

Chronological record of Confinium Mono’s original outline studies, curve-geometry corrections, and optical parameter decisions. All studies remain preserved and reproducible from blank-source UFOs.

1. Variable Pilot (9 Glyphs) 697 advance · 100–700 wght

Established an upright geometric monospace with 1,000 UPM, 697-unit advance, 700 cap height, 500 x-height, and Thin / Book / Bold masters for H O 0 Q g y c e s.

  • Variable Pilot Specimen
  • Original v1 Nine-Glyph Sketch (SVG) · Updated Nine-Glyph Sketch (SVG)

2. Analytic Curve Defect Corrections c / e / s / g

Visual inspection and curve-continuity checks caught degenerate handles and drifting inner corner centres in c, e, s, and g. Outlines were rebuilt using analytic tangent geometry and affine-compatible construction so tangency holds across interpolated weights.

  • Curves Before Correction (PNG) vs Curves After Correction (PNG)

3. Grid & Token-Driven Bold Direction Study 800 advance · 1u = 80

Explored an 800 × 800 cell with 160-unit vertical stems, 160-unit outer radii, and non-concentric tight inner fillets across three coupled treatments (Grid, Open, Dense) for H O 0 Q C E S G Y.

  • Grid / Open / Dense Specimen · Enlarged C/E/S/G Detail
  • Grid Overlays: Grid · Open · Dense

4. Right-Descending 45° Q-Tail Direction Q45 comparison

Evaluated replacing the centred vertical Q tail with a 45° right-descending tail ending at y = −120 with a horizontal cut, resolving a boolean winding cancellation at the bowl attachment.

  • Centred vs 45° Right-Descending Q-Tail

5. Optical Diagonal Thickness & Baseline Promotion Q = 128 · Y = 144

Compared 128, 144, and 160 normal units for the 45° Q tail and Y arms against the lighter reference. Selected Q = 128 and Y = 144 independently and promoted both to the Grid & Token-Driven Bold baseline.

  • Optical Diagonals Comparison (128 / 144 / 160) · Enlarged O/Q/H/Y Detail
  • Grid Overlays: 128 · 144 · 160

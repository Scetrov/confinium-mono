# Steeper K/k and Y review

[Open the side-by-side proof](index.html). Rebuild with `uv run --locked python scripts/k_steeper_study.py`. The pinned control in `reference/` is the current family after the capital-W crown correction (SHA-256 `7f266be718e1c0970a9998222b04b45f74239899e597676ff8c91852f34ca174`). This archived proof preserves the comparison used to approve the `K/k` forks for 0.2.1.

The exterior fork moves to x=475 for `K` and x=470 for `k`. In Bold, the capital upper arm goes from about 51° to 61° from horizontal; its leg goes from about 54° to 61°. The revised lowercase `k` moves from about 45° to 52° on both arms in Bold. The narrower reach is deliberate: a steeper arm needs less horizontal run at the same height. The new lowercase arms mirror one another and grow from 128 to 144 normal units in Bold; the bridge grows from 120 to 140 units. The uppercase candidate is unchanged. All glyphs still advance 800 units. The [first fork proof](first-fork.html) retains the earlier geometry and embedded controls.

`Ķ/ķ` inherit the Latin outlines and retain their marks. Cyrillic `К` shares `K`; Cyrillic `к` gets the same fork treatment but keeps its x-height stem rather than taking the Latin ascender. `A/V` remain unchanged.

## Y review

The current `Y` arms measure about 49 units perpendicular to the stroke at 100, 60–61 at 175, 95–97 at 400, and 144 at 700. Thin `X` is about 54 units. In the 16 px proof, the lighter `Y` still reads cleanly alongside `X`, and its arms don't show the taper seen in the old `K`. I recommend keeping `Y` unchanged rather than matching the Thin `X` width numerically. `Ý Ŷ Ÿ Ỳ` are shown as unchanged controls.

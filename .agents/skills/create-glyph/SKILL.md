# Creating a Confinium Mono glyph

## First identify the track

- **Grid/token Bold study**: `studies/grid-token-bold/`, generated by `scripts/grid_token_study.py`; 1,000 UPM, 800-unit advance and cap height, 1u = 80 units. This is the primary track for A–Z and 0–9 glyph work.
- **Original variable experiment**: `sources/` and `scripts/draw.py`; 1,000 UPM, 697-unit advance, 700 cap height. Do not apply the study's 800-unit coordinates to this track.
- Use the named track, master, variant, or specimen to identify the target. A shared glyph name is not enough. Ask before editing if the target is ambiguous.
- Inspect the relevant UFO, generator, parameters, neighboring glyphs, tests, and existing proofs before editing. Never use third-party font outlines or font images as drawing inputs.

## Grid/token Bold metrics and style

- 1,000 UPM; advance and cap height 800; active bounds x=80…720; baseline y=0, cap y=800; line metrics 920 / −200 / 0; no overshoot.
- Shared vertical stem and outer radius are 160 units (2u). Keep counters generous and maintain the established geometric, faceted construction.
- Three coupled treatments: Grid (bar 140, fillet 32), Open (136, 40), Dense (144, 24). Read `studies/grid-token-bold/parameters.json` and the generator for exact configuration; don't mix tokens between treatments.
- 45° primary diagonals are optically corrected, not mechanically set to the 160-unit vertical stem. Use `diag_bar()` and match neighboring forms. Typical specified normal thickness is 144; W (and the narrow 1 diagonal where applicable) uses 128. Do not assume every diagonal has the same thickness.
- Q tail is a 128-unit normal, 45° stroke descending to the right: `x + y = 532`, y=120…−120, horizontal cut. It may extend to x≈742.51, beyond the active x=720 bound but within the cell. Preserve its direction and intentional overhang.
- Y arms use 144-unit normal thickness, fixed outer silhouette, and a 160-unit lower stem. No junction taper has been approved.
- B, 3, and 8 use a **single outer contour** with a rounded 40-unit waist notch/indent: 28-unit outer-lobe curves and 10-unit inner waist fillets. Avoid overlapping arc construction, sharp re-entrant corners, and pinched walls. Keep the two lobes balanced and counters open.
- Follow established architectural solutions: Z is a single 10-vertex 45° stroke; 2 uses five bands like S; 1 has a 140-unit horizontal top-left flag; 4 is open-top; K arms anchor across y=low…high on the stem to avoid a narrow neck.

## Geometry correctness

- **Join diagonals directly to stems.** When the outer bend is fixed to a grid line, offset the inner diagonal in parallel by the intended **normal** stroke thickness, then intersect it directly with the inner stem wall. Let the inner join land off-grid if needed. Forcing both bends onto the same line or adding an intermediate flare can make the join look pinched or notched. If a crossbar cuts off the join, try moving the bar without thinning it. Check the actual contour vertices, then compare on-grid and at reading sizes (14–18 px).
- **Reference experiment:** [A/V midline knees](../../../studies/grid-token-bold/av-knees/index.html) keeps the outer bends at `y=400` and the stems at `160` units. With `152`-unit parallel diagonals, the inner joins land at `y≈371` on A and `y≈429` on V. A’s `140`-unit crossbar moves down `40` units to clear the join. The approved straight joins are promoted in the full family; the original Grid study remains archived. These measurements are not universal thickness tokens.

- In `BooleanGlyph.removeOverlap()`, winding is significant: every solid outer contour counter-clockwise; every hole clockwise. Use the script's `ccw_poly()`, `rect()`, and `diag_bar()` helpers; reverse hole contours explicitly. Wrongly wound solids can silently subtract and create cutaways.
- Prefer a clean continuous contour over overlapping primitives when the outline has a designed waist or complex join. Check that unions, counters, and component overlaps survive overlap removal.
- Keep glyph advance and side bearings consistent with the cell and neighboring glyphs. Respect intentional overshoots/overhangs only where specified; do not change global metrics to accommodate one outline.
- Preserve existing codepoint-to-glyph mappings unless changing them is explicitly in scope. Do not promote unapproved variants.

## Variable-weight standards

- **Start at Thin, not just Book.** The generic inset is a starting point, not an optical pass. Independent edge insets can tilt stems and terminals, warp narrow cutouts, or create wedge-shaped diagonals. If that happens, construct a compatible Thin master from shared stem and bar positions and intersections of parallel edges (`scripts/variable_lowercase.py`, `scripts/variable_diagonals.py`). Keep the approved Bold outline exact unless Bold is explicitly in scope.
- **Measure the intended relationships.** Check both edges of each baseline, x-height, cap-height, or descender terminal; test vertical/horizontal lands through interpolation, not just bounding boxes. Measure diagonal thickness *normal to the stroke*, at both ends and away from the join. Check the inner and outer curve tangents and wall thickness through the turn, not just the controls or extrema. Equalize strokes intended to match, not every element globally: optical differences in diagonals, bars, dots, or multi-stroke letters can be deliberate.
- **Proof related forms together.** Compare `h/n` shoulders, `i/j` stems, dots, and top bars, `g/j/y` descender turns, and the top and bottom of `s`. Check `k/x` endpoints and diagonal balance, and compare `v/w` with `vv/w`: align `v`’s inner point with **both upper-facing valleys** of `w`, not with its central notch rising from the baseline. Use the approved fuller lowercase `w` as the reference; preserve its centre strokes and open valleys. Also review the relevant uppercase forms (e.g. `M/N/W`, `H/Ĥ/Н`) rather than assuming the same problem affects them.
- **Diagnose optical spacing in words.** Measure shaped advances and offsets, ink bounds, and inter-glyph ink gaps at relevant heights separately. Keep the cell fixed when the problem is optical; equal bounds alone do not close an open channel between stems. Compare the reported word and neighboring pairs at 14–16 px and enlarged across the affected weights. See [approved family relationships](../../../AGENTS.md#variable-outline-standards).
- **Carry changes through real dependencies.** Identify shared-script outlines, accented and dotless forms, and deliberate transforms (such as a shortened accented ascender). A flattened accented base can drift even when the plain glyph is correct. Reuse the corrected base geometry at each master, preserve mark contours/placement and intended transforms, and compare precomposed versus decomposed shaping and advances. Don’t treat visually similar but independently drawn glyphs as aliases.
- **Verify from the compiled font.** Proof large outlines against the grid, then at 14–16 px in words and confusable pairs. For light-end work include `100`, an under-200 instance such as `175`, `400`, and `700`; add intermediate coordinates as needed. Check baseline/height alignment, width consistency, open counters, and contours separately. Passing alignment or area checks is not evidence of uniform stroke widths across the whole alphabet.

## Work in phases

### 1. Scope the batch

- Identify the track and affected variants or masters. For an expansion, record the exact `U+XXXX → glyphName` mappings, expected glyph classes (including intentionally blank glyphs), and any class-specific bounds or shaping behavior. Group larger requests into batches that can be built and proofed independently.
- Agree on the repertoire before drawing. If asked only to plan, or if the track, mappings, or intended metrics are unclear, propose a batch and ask only for the missing decisions. Do not start the next batch unless it is in scope.
- Capture the baseline: current coverage, a relevant proof, and the files that control sources, tests, and display. For Grid, check `scripts/grid_token_study.py` (including `shapes(p)`, glyph order, and Unicode assignment), `studies/grid-token-bold/coverage.json`, `tests/test_grid_token.py`, and `scripts/web.py`. Check derived studies only to determine whether they are in scope; don't regenerate them by default.

### 2. Draw and integrate

- Follow the track's existing helpers and conventions. Add the approved glyphs to every affected variant or master. For the variable experiment, keep master contours and components interpolation-compatible. Update the in-scope coverage metadata, tests, and specimen generators together; make sure an encoded glyph cannot silently pass with an empty outline unless it is intentionally blank.
- For a **generator edit**, inspect existing UFOs and overlays first. `draw` overwrites them. Regenerate in a scratch copy when manual edits or archive preservation are at risk; review the diff and apply only intended changes. Never silently discard manual edits. For a **direct UFO edit**, skip `draw` and check whether the generator also needs updating so a future regeneration preserves the edit.
- Build from the updated UFOs using `uv run python scripts/grid_token_study.py build` or `uv run python scripts/build.py`. `build` does not regenerate outlines. Preserve the deterministic build defaults.

### 3. Verify and hand off

- Keep comparison controls pinned; never use the evolving full family as an archived “before” font. Save dated Chromium captures alongside their exact proof inputs or identifiable hashes and revisions. Preserve earlier captures and label any reconstruction explicitly.
- Promote only approved variants. Apply the selected outlines through the full-family builder, including affected style adaptations and derived glyphs; verify variable Bold equals Full Grid. Audit earlier approved decisions (such as Q128 and Y144) independently of unselected studies. Rebuild live Explorer/specimen assets, [refresh affected Hugo fragments and check the served fonts](../../../AGENTS.md#how-to-build-the-static-website), and keep old links and proofs usable.

- Run `uv run python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` after outline, script, or parameter changes. Use the proof commands in `AGENTS.md`; inspect their output paths first and stage new captures separately if running them would overwrite verification archives.
- Render the added or changed glyphs in isolation and alongside neighboring forms, in representative text where applicable, at intended sizes. Check joins, counters, weight balance, and spacing. Use the compiled font's coverage and shaping results to rule out fallback before judging a browser proof.
- Report the batch scope, actual coverage delta, affected variants or masters, checks run, proof paths inspected, and any remaining blockers. If a required check can't be run, report the batch as unverified, not done.

## Definition of done

A batch is done only when all applicable checks pass:

- **Scope and source parity:** Every affected UFO has the intended names, mappings, and outlines; the compiled `cmap` delta exactly matches the scoped changes (empty for an outline-only edit). Existing mappings and out-of-scope tracks or variants are unchanged. Coverage metadata and published samples agree with the binaries they describe.
- **Geometry and metrics:** Every visible addition has nonempty contours after overlap removal and compilation; only intentionally blank glyphs are empty. Existing metrics, bounds, and documented exceptions still pass. New classes have explicit bounds checks rather than weakening existing assertions. Single-cell additions have the track's fixed advance; any intentionally different behavior is specified and tested.
- **Shaping and interpolation:** New mappings shape to the expected glyph IDs, not `.notdef` or fallback, with the intended advances in isolation and representative runs for every affected build. For variable work, masters are compatible, and the compiled variable font is checked at master locations and at least one intermediate weight.
- **Regression and visual evidence:** The full test suite passes, and rendered proofs from the rebuilt font are visually inspected. Existing studies, unrelated UFO edits, and verification archives are preserved. Do not claim production readiness or invent licensing claims.
